## Name
Moises Keyboard Shortcuts

## Short description (132 chars max)
Keyboard shortcuts for Moises.ai — press V, D, B, O to instantly mute Vocals, Drums, Bass, and Other tracks while practicing.

## Detailed description

Practice music without breaking your flow. This extension adds keyboard shortcuts to Moises.ai's stem player so you can mute and unmute tracks instantly — no mouse needed.

Shortcuts:
• V — Toggle Vocals mute
• D — Toggle Drums mute
• B — Toggle Bass mute
• O — Toggle Other mute

How it works:
Just open any song in the Moises.ai player (studio.moises.ai) and press a key. A small notification confirms the action. That's it — no setup, no configuration, no accounts.

Designed for musicians:
When you're practicing along with a track, reaching for the mouse to click a tiny mute button breaks your concentration. With this extension, you keep your hands on your instrument and toggle tracks with a single keypress.

Smart and safe:
• Shortcuts are disabled when you're typing in a text field
• Ctrl+V, Cmd+V, Alt+V still work normally (paste, etc.)
• No data collected — everything runs locally in your browser
